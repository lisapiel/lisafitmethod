Common questions
What you should know.
See all questions →
How long is each training session?
Between 45 and 60 minutes including warm-up and cool-down. Some days run a little shorter.
Do I need a gym?
You need access to basic equipment: dumbbells, a resistance band, and a flat bench or equivalent. A commercial gym works, but a well-equipped home gym is fine too.
Is this for complete beginners?
Yes. It&#x27;s built for people new to structured training or who&#x27;ve been winging it for years. You don&#x27;t need prior experience with any of the movements — everything is taught from the ground up.
Can I run it more than once?
That&#x27;s the point. The program is designed to be repeated. Each round you add weight and progress further. Most people who see real results run it 2 or 3 times.
Is it a subscription?
No. One-time payment for each course. You pay once and keep ongoing access.
Can men use this program?
Yes. The movements and principles apply to everyone. There are no gender-specific requirements.
